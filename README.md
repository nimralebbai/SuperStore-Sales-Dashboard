# SuperStore-Sales-Dashboard
Interactive SuperStore Sales Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze sales, profit, orders, quantity, category, ship mode, payment mode, region, state, subcategory, and segment performance.


![SuperStore Sales Dashboard](dashboard.PNG)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is a **SuperStore Sales Dashboard** created using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance and helps analyze sales, profit, orders, quantity, categories, subcategories, regions, shipping modes, payment modes, states, and customer segments.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track total sales, orders, profit, and quantity
- Compare sales across different product categories
- Analyze sales by subcategory
- Understand sales performance by shipping mode
- Analyze different payment methods
- Compare sales performance across regions
- Analyze state-wise sales and profit
- Compare sales across customer segments
- Analyze monthly profit trends year-over-year

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Category

Shows sales performance across:

- Office Supplies
- Technology
- Furniture

### 2. Sales by Ship Mode

Analyzes sales based on:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day

### 3. Sales by Payment Mode

Shows the contribution of different payment methods:

- Cards
- Online
- COD

### 4. Sales and Profit by State

The map visualization helps understand the geographical distribution of sales and profit across different states.

### 5. Sales by Subcategory

Shows sales performance for important subcategories such as:

- Phones
- Chairs
- Binders

### 6. Monthly Profit YOY

Compares monthly profit performance across:

- 2019
- 2020

This helps identify monthly trends and changes in profitability.

### 7. Sales by Segment

Compares sales across:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 8. Region Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- Central
- East
- South
- West

These filters allow users to analyze the dashboard based on selected regions.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard provides a clear overview of SuperStore's business performance. It helps identify the strongest product categories and subcategories, compare different shipping and payment methods, understand regional and state-wise performance, and analyze sales across different customer segments.

The year-over-year profit analysis also helps understand monthly changes in profitability and identify periods of stronger or weaker performance.
