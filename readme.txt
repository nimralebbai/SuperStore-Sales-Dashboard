# SuperStore Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is a Power BI dashboard created to analyze SuperStore sales data and understand overall business performance.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales, profit, orders, quantity, categories, regions, shipping modes, payment methods, subcategories, and customer segments.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze total sales and profit
- Track total orders and quantity sold
- Compare sales across different regions
- Analyze sales by category and subcategory
- Understand different shipping modes
- Analyze payment methods
- Compare performance by customer segment
- Identify monthly profit trends
- Understand state-wise sales performance

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX
- Data Visualization

## 📌 Dashboard KPIs

- Total Sales: 1.57M
- Total Orders: 5.90K
- Total Profit: 175.26K
- Total Quantity: 22K

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- KPI Cards
- Region Filters
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Payment Mode Analysis
- Sales & Profit by State
- Sales by Subcategory
- Monthly Profit YOY Analysis
- Sales by Customer Segment

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify high-performing categories, regions, and subcategories while providing a clear view of sales and profit trends. It also helps compare customer segments, payment methods, and shipping modes to understand overall business performance.
